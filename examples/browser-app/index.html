CIRCULess browser app — example

Signs in, lists a tenant's resources, uploads and downloads a CSV, and has a service on the node extract its column names. The interesting part is how it gets its tokens.

Resources

Listing a tenant's resources is a management call: it answers "what have I registered", and only an administrator of that organisation may ask. You will get a 403 for an organisation you do not administer — that is correct. A consumer discovers another organisation's service through the catalogue, which is filtered by what its owner chose to publish, never by reading their registry.
